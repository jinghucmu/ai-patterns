## Why this site exists

An AI capability does nothing for a person until someone works out how they ask it, how they check what it returns, and how they recover when it is wrong. That work is separate from building the model, and it is the work I do. Over the past few years it has turned into a handful of shipped products, and each one taught me something about designing with AI that I kept wanting to tell other designers. This site is where I write those things down in a form other teams can reuse, whatever they are building.

## What is here

Design patterns from products I have designed, each with what users needed, the problem, the design, what made it hard and how it fails. The patterns are grouped into two themes: AI across modes, and AI for accessibility.

## How to use it

You are free to apply anything here in your own product. If you have built something that solves one of these problems differently, I would like to hear about it.
