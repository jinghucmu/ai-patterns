## Why this site exists

I help people shop smartly with videos. Over the past few years that one sentence has turned into a handful of products, and each one taught me something about designing with AI that I kept wanting to tell other designers. This site is where I write those things down in a form other teams can reuse.

## What is here

Design patterns from products I have designed, each with the problem, the design, what made it hard and how it fails. The patterns are grouped into three themes: AI in shopping, AI across modes, and AI for accessibility.

## How to use it

You are free to apply anything here in your own product. If you have built something that solves one of these problems differently, I would like to hear about it.
